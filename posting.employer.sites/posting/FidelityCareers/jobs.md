Description

Do you want to work on leading edge cloud technologies that are transforming how we scale our next generation platforms?

This is an opportunity for a highly motivated Principal Cloud Engineer to join our Observability Platform team within Fidelity Architecture and Engineering. You will work in a diverse, open and transparent culture where you will contribute to powering the next generation of Fidelity’s digital services through innovative solutions using the latest cloud native technologies and leading-edge engineering practices.

We believe that empowered and self-motivated teams can achieve outstanding things. We are passionate about opensource contribution, sharing our expertise and knowledge with the engineering community while adopting a continuous learning approach support by a dedicated learning day each week.

The Experience We’re Looking For

Extensive experience working with Python and at least one other language.
Extensive experience working with AWS or Azure.
Extensive experience working with Kubernetes and Kubernetes ecosystem.
Extensive experience working hands-on with infrastructure as code.
An advocate for delivering software using the latest engineering best practices and principles.
Strong mentoring, coaching skills and communication skills.
Passion for new technologies and working in an agile environment.
Bonus Skills

Working knowledge of Go.
Experience working with observability platforms.
Experience working with the opensource community.
The Team

This is an opportunity for a highly motivated Principal Cloud Engineer to join Fidelity Architecture and Engineering, who are responsible for enabling the next generation cloud platforms across Fidelity. We excel in the construction of high quality, secure and scalable cloud platforms using industry-leading engineering principles and practices. We are a global team which has a passion for open source and continuous learning while pursuing engineering excellence in the cloud in a collaborative, open and transparent culture.
